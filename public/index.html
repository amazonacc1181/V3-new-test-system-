INVIGILATION & COMMUNICATION

Live test monitor

Monitor active attempts and communicate with one student or all current participants.

Students currently taking tests

Students are shown a consent notice before the test. Once consent is given, an administrator can start an audio/video call during that active test. Students see the call status and can mute, disable their camera, or end live calls. Calls are peer-to-peer and not recorded by this app. Connections may fail on some networks because the free setup has no TURN relay.
